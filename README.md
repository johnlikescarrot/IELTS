# IELTS Master Course — 12-Hour Video Series + Complete Preparation Book

This repository contains the **IELTS 12 Hours Full Course for Beginners (2023)** — a
Master-Course of all 4 components (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking) in 12 video
parts (~57.5 minutes each, ~11.5 hours total) — **plus** the complete preparation book
compiled from studying every part of the course.

## The Book (PDF)

**[`IELTS-Master-Course-Book.pdf`](IELTS-Master-Course-Book.pdf)** — *IELTS Master Course:
The Complete Preparation Book (2026 Edition).* 15 chapters, 100+ pages:

| Part | Chapters | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| A — The Test | 1–2 | Test formats, scoring, study plans (12-week / 4-week / 7-day), diagnostics |
| B — Listening | 3–5 | Skills & method, every question type worked, 6 practice sets with tapescripts & keys |
| C — Reading | 6–8 | Skimming/scanning/synonyms, every question type worked, 5 practice passages & keys |
| D — Speaking | 9–10 | 4 criteria, Parts 1–3 method, 60 cue cards, 100+ questions, 9 sample talks |
| E — Writing | 11–13 | Scoring, sentence grammar, Task 1 reports + GT letters, Task 2 essays, 100 prompts |
| F — Language & Exam Day | 14 | Grammar, 500-item topic vocabulary, idioms, exam-day checklists |
| G — Answers & Trackers | 15 | All answer keys, checklists, error logs, video-course index |

Sources: `book/*.md` (Markdown) + `book/build_pdf.py` (ReportLab renderer).
Rebuild with: `pip install reportlab pypdf Pillow && python3 book/build_pdf.py`

## The Videos (12 parts)

| Part | Content |
|---|---|

| 12 | Writing: Task 1 graph practice, GT letters, full Task 2 essays |

## How to Use

1. Read Chapters 1–2, take the diagnostic, pick a study plan.
2. Work module by module (Chapters 3–13), watching the matching video parts.
3. Practise actively: cover answers, attempt first, log every error (Chapter 15).
4. Finish with Chapter 14 (grammar, vocabulary, exam-day checklists).

*Book compiled from full transcription and slide analysis of all 12 video parts.
All practice material is original, created to train the skills the videos teach.*
