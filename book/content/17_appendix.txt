# Appendix B — Additional Model Answers

## A Model Advantages/Disadvantages Essay

**Question:** *Some people believe that studying at university is the best route to a successful career, while others think it is better to start work straight after school. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*

<<<
People have different views about whether a university education or an early start in employment is the better path to a successful career. While working straight after school has clear advantages for some, I believe that, for most people, a university degree offers the stronger foundation.

On the one hand, there are good reasons to start work immediately. Some young people learn best by doing, and industries such as skilled trades, hospitality and information technology often value practical experience more than formal qualifications. Entering the workforce early also means earning a salary sooner, avoiding years of tuition fees, and gaining a head start in climbing the career ladder. For someone with a clear vocational goal, a degree can even feel like an unnecessary delay.

On the other hand, a university education develops skills that are hard to acquire on the job. It teaches critical thinking, independent research and the ability to communicate complex ideas, all of which are prized by employers in professional fields such as medicine, law, engineering and academia. Furthermore, graduates, on average, still enjoy higher lifetime earnings and better job security, and universities provide the networks that open doors later in a career.

In my opinion, the best choice depends on the individual and the career they want. However, for anyone aiming at a profession that requires specialist knowledge, university is not merely useful but essential. A sensible approach is for students to weigh their goals carefully and, where possible, combine study with part-time work or internships.

In conclusion, although starting work straight after school suits some people and some careers, I believe a university education remains the most reliable route to long-term success for the majority.
>>>

## A Model Part 3 Answer (Festivals)

<<<
Q: Are there any kinds of festivals that are becoming more popular in your country?
A: Yes, I think music and food festivals are becoming noticeably more popular, especially among young people. Generally speaking, these events are heavily promoted on social media, so they attract large crowds from all over the country. I believe this reflects a broader trend: people today value experiences over possessions, and festivals offer a chance to enjoy live entertainment and try new food in a lively atmosphere. That said, some traditional religious festivals are losing their original spirit, as they have become more commercial over time.
>>>

# Appendix C — Glossary of IELTS Terms

[[table:Glossary]]
| Term | Meaning |
| Band score | Your result on the 9-level scale (whole or half bands) |
| Raw score | Number of correct answers (out of 40) in Listening/Reading |
| Overall band | The average of the four component scores, rounded to the nearest half band |
| Task Achievement | Criterion: did you fully complete Writing Task 1? |
| Task Response | Criterion: did you fully answer the essay question? |
| Coherence | Logical ordering of ideas |
| Cohesion | How ideas are linked with connectors, pronouns, synonyms |
| Lexical Resource | Vocabulary range and accuracy |
| Grammatical Range & Accuracy | Sentence variety and grammatical correctness |
| Overview | The summary sentence(s) of main trends in a Task 1 report |
| Thesis statement | The sentence stating your position in an essay introduction |
| Topic sentence | The sentence stating the main idea of a paragraph |
| Paraphrase | Expressing the same meaning in different words |
| Skimming | Fast reading for the main idea |
| Scanning | Fast reading to find specific information |
| Distractor | A wrong option that sounds right |
| Cue card | The topic card you speak about in Speaking Part 2 |
