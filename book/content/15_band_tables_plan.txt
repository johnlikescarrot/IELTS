# 19. Band Scores and Study Plans

## Your Target Band Decides the Plan

Before planning, write down three numbers: your **current band** (use a full practice test), your **target band**, and the **date** of your test. The gap between current and target, divided by the “100 hours per half band” rule of thumb, tells you how many hours you need — and the date tells you how many per week.

[[table:Study hours needed (rule of thumb)]]
| Improvement | Approximate hours |
| +0.5 band | ~100 hours |
| +1.0 band | ~200 hours |
| +1.5 bands | ~300 hours |
| +2.0 bands | ~400 hours |

> NOTE: These are averages for focused study. Some learners move faster, some slower. The point is to **plan realistically** — a candidate at Band 5 who wants Band 6.5 in one month needs to rethink either the target or the date.

## How the Overall Band Is Calculated (Recap)

Overall = average of the four component scores, rounded to the nearest half band: .25 → up to .5; .75 → up to the next whole band; .125 → down.

**Strategy tip from the table:** your weakest component drags the average. Often the fastest overall gain comes from the component where you are losing the most marks — for most candidates that is **Writing** or **Speaking**.

## A Four-Week Intensive Plan (for a test one month away)

[[table:Four-week intensive plan]]
| Week | Focus |
| Week 1 | Foundations: Chapters 1–2, Listening technique (Ch. 3–4), 2 full Listening tests, learn 50 synonyms |
| Week 2 | Reading: Chapters 6–8, one passage a day with the four-step method, 2 full Reading tests, learn topic vocabulary |
| Week 3 | Writing: Chapters 10–14, one Task 1 and one Task 2 every day, study the model answers, learn trend language and linkers |
| Week 4 | Speaking: Chapters 15–17, record yourself daily, one full mock test, review checklists and band tables |

## An Eight-Week Plan (the recommended pace)

[[table:Eight-week plan]]
| Weeks | Focus |
| 1–2 | IELTS overview, scores, Listening (all chapters + 3 tests), vocabulary foundation |
| 3–4 | Reading (all chapters + 3 tests), daily skimming/scanning drills, synonym bank |
| 5–6 | Writing Task 1 + Task 2 (all chapters + 6 essays with self-marking), grammar toolkit |
| 7 | Speaking (all chapters + daily recording), model-answer study |
| 8 | Full mock tests (2), timed, then review every mistake against the checklists |

## Weekly Routine That Works

+ **Daily (30–60 min):** vocabulary (10 new words + review), 15 minutes of listening, read one news article and skim/scan it.
+ **Every two days:** one timed Reading passage or one Writing task, self-marked against the descriptors.
+ **Weekly:** one full timed Listening test and one full timed Reading test; record yourself speaking twice.

## A Note on Motivation

The source course closes with its students’ own testimony: the test is **attainable** — “everything is attainable if we do the effort.” Band 9 is possible, half-band improvements are real, and the difference between candidates is almost always **organised, consistent practice**, not talent. Plan, then practise.
