## 15.6 Glossary of IELTS Terms

**Academic module:** the test version for university study and professional registration. **Band (score):** your 0–9 level per module and overall. **Coherence:** logical organisation of ideas. **Cohesion:** grammatical linking (linkers, reference words). **Completion:** gap-fill tasks (form, note, table, sentence, summary, diagram). **Distractor:** plausible-but-wrong information planted in Listening/Reading. **Enquiry on Results (EOR):** paid remarking service. **Fluency:** smooth, natural-paced speech. **General Training (GT):** the test version for migration and work. **Lexical Resource:** vocabulary range and accuracy (Writing/Speaking criterion). **Matching:** tasks pairing items (headings, features, endings). **Multiple choice:** A/B/C(/D) selection tasks. **One Skill Retake:** resitting one module within 60 days (selected centres). **Overview:** the summary paragraph in Task 1 (no detailed figures). **Paraphrase:** same meaning, different words — the core of IELTS. **TFNG:** True/False/Not Given (Reading). **Task Achievement:** Task 1 writing criterion (did you cover the visual?). **Task Response:** Task 2 writing criterion (did you answer the question?). **Thesis (statement):** your position sentence in a Task 2 introduction. **Word limit:** maximum words per answer (e.g., NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS). **YNNG:** Yes/No/Not Given (writer's views).
